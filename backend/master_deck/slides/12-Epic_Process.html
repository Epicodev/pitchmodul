"The Epic Process"

This is how a typical collaboration proceeds. A thoroughly tested process that ensures quality, transparency, and the right match every time.

1

Request

Key Account Manager receives your request and schedules a meeting

2

Candidate search

Dedicated Resource Manager starts the search in our global database

3

Candidate screening

Candidates are interviewed and previous job references are screened

4

Candidate selection

Candidates are selected on qualifications and personality to match your profile

5

CV to client

All candidates' CVs are sent to you for review

6

Interview

Interview with the candidate, you, and Epico

7

Contract signed

The contract is signed and the consultant starts
